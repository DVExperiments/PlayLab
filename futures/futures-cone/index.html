How it works

  1. Choose a set of scenarios, or add your own.
  2. Place each one in the cone depending on how likely it seems by 2040, and note why.
  3. Star the scenarios you’d want to happen. These are your preferable futures, and they can sit anywhere in the cone.
  4. Look at the pattern you’ve made, then reflect on what it says about your assumptions.
