Futures thinking

Signal Collider

Draw random mixes of signals, settings, people and objects, then imagine everyday life in the future.

Time
10 minutes
You leave with
A collection of future artefacts

How it works

  1. Draw a set of cards. Together they describe a moment in the future.
  2. Imagine the object the cards describe. Give it a name and describe it in a few sentences. Go with your first idea.
  3. Save it, keep any cards you like, and swap the rest to try another mix.
  4. Aim for three ideas or more, then reflect on what they reveal.
