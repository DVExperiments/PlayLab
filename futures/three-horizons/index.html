Map what’s fading, what’s emerging and what you want to grow in your own field.

Time
20 minutes
You leave with

How it works

  1. Name the area you want to explore, such as how your sector delivers a service.
  2. Horizon 1: describe how things work now, and mark what’s worth keeping.
  3. Horizon 3: describe the future you want, and where you already see hints of it.
  4. Horizon 2: list the changes and experiments in between. Decide whether each one leads towards Horizon 3 or props up Horizon 1.
  5. Look at the map and reflect on where to put your energy.
