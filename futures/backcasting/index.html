Backcasting Timeline

Start from a future you want and work backwards to what needs to happen today.

Time
20 minutes
You leave with
A step-by-step timeline

How it works

  1. Describe a future you want as if it has already happened, and how you’d know you’d got there.
  2. Step back in time. For each date, ask: what had to be true by then for the next step to happen?
  3. When you reach today, decide on a first step you can take in the next three months.
  4. Read your timeline from today forwards to check it makes sense, then reflect.
