Futures Wheel

Map the ripple effects of a change, out to the consequences of its consequences.

Time
15 minutes
You leave with
A consequences map

How it works

  1. Write a change at the centre: a trend, a technology or a decision. The more specific, the better.
  2. Add direct consequences. What happens because of this change?
  3. For each one, keep asking “and then what?” to add second and third-order consequences.
  4. Mark each consequence as positive, negative or uncertain, and notice who it’s positive or negative for.
  5. Reflect on what you found, then download your wheel.
