How it works

  1. Read the task, then both versions. One was produced by AI and one by a person.
  2. Check each version against the six questions. Judge the work, not who you think wrote it.
  3. Decide which you’d actually use, and guess which was AI. Then reveal the answer and the notes.
  4. Work through each pair, or add your own, then look at the patterns and reflect.
