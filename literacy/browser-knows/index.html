Beyond this page

What other websites can also see

  • Your IP address, which reveals your internet provider and often your town or city.
  • Tracking code from advertising and analytics companies, which can follow you from site to site.
  • Whether you’re logged in to services like Google or Facebook, when their buttons or sign-in tools appear on a page.
  • Session replay tools, which can record every movement, click and scroll on a page, sometimes including what you type before you press send.

What you can do

  • Use a browser with built-in tracking protection, such as Firefox, Safari or Brave, and try its stricter settings.
  • Add a content blocker such as uBlock Origin to stop many trackers from loading.
  • Keep your browser up to date and go easy on unusual add-ons, which can make you stand out more.
  • See how your browser compares with thousands of others at Cover Your Tracks or AmIUnique.
  • Under the Privacy Act 2020, you can ask any New Zealand organisation what personal information it holds about you. The Office of the Privacy Commissioner explains how.
