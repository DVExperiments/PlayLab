Stakeholder Ecosystem Map

Map who a technology touches, including the people and places easily left out.

Time
20 minutes
You leave with
A stakeholder map

How it works

  1. Name the technology, service or change at the centre.
  2. Add the people, groups and places it touches, and place each in a ring.
  3. Use the “often left out” suggestions to find who you’ve missed.
  4. For each one, note whether they gain or lose, how much say they have, and whether they’ve been asked.
  5. Look at who carries the costs with little say, then reflect.

Who does it touch?

A technology or change from your own work is best.

Often left out

Also ask: who are the kaitiaki, the guardians, of the places, people, knowledge or data this touches? Have they been part of the conversation?
