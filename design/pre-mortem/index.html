Pre-mortem

Imagine your project has failed, then work out why before it happens.

Time
15 minutes
You leave with
A list of risks and responses

How it works

  1. Describe your project and when it should be a success.
  2. Jump forward in time. It has failed. Write the headline.
  3. List every reason it might have failed. Use the lenses to find blind spots, and don’t hold back.
  4. Rate how likely and how damaging each reason is.
  5. For the biggest risks, decide on an early warning sign and what you’ll do now.
