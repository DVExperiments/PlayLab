How Might We Reframer

Turn one problem statement into many by stretching, shrinking and flipping it.

Time

How it works

  1. Describe a problem you care about, then write it as a first “How might we…” question.
  2. Work through the reframing moves. Try at least five, and go with your first thought.
  3. Shortlist the questions that open up the most interesting possibilities.
  4. Choose one, check it against the list, and reflect on how your thinking shifted.

Start with the problem

Describe what’s happening and who it affects. A real problem from your work is best.

Don’t polish it. This is just your starting point.
