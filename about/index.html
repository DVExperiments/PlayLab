About the PlayLab

The Futures PlayLab is an academyEX space for hands-on experimentation, creative practice and critical engagement with emerging technologies.

Developed alongside the Master of Technological Futures, it’s where ideas about technological futures become things you can try, test and learn from. You don’t need to be a technical expert, and you don’t need a finished project. Bring your curiosity, your questions and your context, and start exploring.

Getting the most from the tools

Each tool takes between 5 and 20 minutes, and you can use them in any order. They work best when you bring something real: a decision your organisation is facing, a technology changing your sector, or a project you’re part of. Most tools end with a few reflection questions you can draw on in class discussions, assessments or your own practice.

Every tool lets you download what you make, either as an image, a save file you can reopen later, or a printout. You can save to PDF from the print option.

Your privacy

These tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to academyEX or anyone else, and there are no accounts or logins.

Your progress is remembered in this browser on this device only. If you clear your browser data, switch devices or use a private window, your work won’t be there, so save a copy of anything you want to keep. If you’re on a shared computer, use the Start over button when you finish.

Suggest a tool or report a problem

The PlayLab grows with the people who use it. If you have an idea for a tool, or something isn’t working, let us know on GitHub or talk to your MTF facilitators.
